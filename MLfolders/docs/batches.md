# The Garnet and Emerald batches

The main hardware dataset: 6 segments, 26 jobs, 1,419,000 shots on IQM Garnet and IQM Emerald, run back to back in the brief's order (`garnet-b1`, `garnet-b2`, `emerald-b1`, `emerald-b2`, `garnet-b3`, `emerald-b3`), so each device's test batch (B3) is time-separated from its own training batches by the other device's work. Driver: `scripts/batch_run.py`. Generated by `scripts/batch_run.py doc`; the notes come from the handoff.

## The outcome

**All 26 jobs ran, 1,419,000 shots; every one PASS.** Each job has exactly one ledger row, written by `hw.submit` and checked against the plan (task, device, calibration set, qubits, `circuits x shots`, job id, purpose with the job label). All 417 circuits passed their sanity checks. Re-pulled read-only by id (`hw.retrieve`), all 26 jobs equal the committed raw data: memory, calibration set and timeline. The calibration sets never moved: Garnet `a18a947f` and Emerald `23e12f6b` in every snapshot, from 00:30 to 01:18 UTC. No job failed, none was resubmitted or recovered, and nothing went to `:mock`, a timeslot or Pulla.

## What ran, and how it was checked

- **Freeze.** Every circuit was rebuilt from the committed snapshot the plan was made from (Garnet a18a947f, Emerald 23e12f6b). Its native, Stim and QASM hashes were checked against `placement.json`, after merging main `79092cf` too: 417 of 417 equal. `circuits/` was never regenerated.
- **Live.** Before each segment, `resonance_check.py` took a live snapshot. Every circuit's own qubits still served `prx` and `measure`, and every `cz` locus was still calibrated (Emerald: `check_live_couplers` too). Then, on the read-only backend, every job's own `create_run_request` was built and IQM validated it against the live architecture. Each circuit's serialized instructions hashed to its `native_sha256`, and its sent qubits were its placement's. `qubit_mapping` was None; heralding none, DD disabled, `active_reset_cycles=None`.
- **One job at a time, in plan order.** The circuits went in `job_plan.json` order, with one `qubit_index_to_name`: the union of the circuits' own maps, which never conflict. Each job's manifest (`raw/…/<label>.json`: position → circuit, `native_sha256`, qubits) was written before `hw.submit`, because IQM returns `circuit_names: [null]`.
- **Sanity, per circuit.** Checked on every circuit:
  - the shape and the all-zero rows;
  - the first readout of every non-data qubit: random ones in [0.4, 0.6], deterministic ones misread under 0.3. In E1/E4 Z basis, X-type ancillas random and Z-type low; X bases the reverse. The pattern is read from a noiseless sample of the circuit's Stim source;
  - the detection fraction under 0.45 (0.48 where the priors themselves predict over 0.3: E4 at 5 µs);
  - `meta.json` `physical_qubits` equal to the plan's.

  Rehearsed offline on the dry runs' Aer records of all 417 circuits: 0 false stops. Inverted or random records fail every circuit; reversed, shifted or dead readouts fail most, and every job holds circuits that catch them.
- **Decoder priors** (the orchestrator's instruction, replacing the brief's pilot priors): `device.hardware_noise(cal, spec)` from main `79092cf`, on both devices. It has the symmetric idle over the compiled readout window plus E4's delay, `measure_fidelity` at the final readout, and no idle after a surface memory's last round. Emerald has no compile file, so its window falls back on `measure_ns`, 360 ns, the probe alone (logged in every record). Every record says `priors: hardware_noise (79092cf)`. Nothing was chosen on test.

## What the data say

- **Garnet against Emerald.** Pooled over all three batches, ε_L(A = 1) is 0.094–0.111 on Garnet (pA, pB; Z, X) and 0.039–0.049 on Emerald (eA, eB): Emerald's per-round error is about 2.3× lower.
- **The fit is a poor description.** Every free-A fit sits at its bound, A = 1. P_L(R = 1) lies far below the single-exponential curve: Garnet 0.024 against about 0.09 from ε = 0.1. The single-point ε climbs from about 0.02–0.03 at R = 1 to about 0.10–0.15 by R = 8 on Garnet, and to 0.045–0.058 by R = 20 on Emerald. Garnet's P_L saturates near 0.5 by R ≈ 15–20. χ²/dof is 27–163 on Garnet and 7–53 on Emerald, so the bootstrap intervals, which assume the model, understate the real uncertainty. The fitted ε is mostly set by the bulk rounds. A fit from R ≥ 2, or a model with a first-round term, is the obvious next analysis.
- **Garnet drifted within the same calibration set.**
  - Test-batch ε_L (B3) is 0.106–0.147, against 0.083–0.115 in B1 and B2.
  - B3's detection fractions run higher too (up to 0.351, against 0.319 in B1), and its Z basis worsened more than X.
  - B3 was taken 25–30 min after B1, on a18a947f throughout. Finetune and test therefore differ in device state, not only in shots: a model fine-tuned on B1+B2 is tested on a noisier Garnet.
  - Emerald's batches agree within their intervals (Z eA 0.050, 0.049, 0.048).
- **X against Z: the idle-dephasing question.**
  - **Garnet, finetune B1+B2:** X/Z = 1.15 on pA (0.1046 [0.1030, 0.1062] against 0.0911 [0.0898, 0.0926]; the intervals don't overlap), and 1.00 on pB.
  - **Garnet, test B3:** X/Z = 0.90 (pA) and 0.72 (pB), where Z worsened.
  - **Garnet, E4 (batch 2, pA):** the direct idle probe. X rises faster than Z at 1 µs and 2 µs: X 0.096 → 0.170 → 0.249, Z 0.084 → 0.130 → 0.179, the E1 value first. That is extra dephasing during idles on pA, which the X-basis memory feels.
  - **Emerald:** X is better than Z, X/Z = 0.80 on eA (0.0389 [0.0381, 0.0397] against 0.0489 [0.0480, 0.0498]) and 0.95 on eB, in every batch. In E4 (eA) both rise at a similar rate: X 0.041 → 0.071 → 0.111, Z 0.049 → 0.088 → 0.129. Emerald's errors are bit-flip-heavy, not dephasing-dominated.
  - **At 5 µs** P_L saturates on both devices, so those fits carry little information (A free drops to 0.18 and 0.25 for Z).
- **The priors against the hardware.**
  - **E1:** detection fractions run 0.87–1.46× the `hardware_noise` prediction on Garnet, and 0.87–1.75× on Emerald. The ratio grows with R (Garnet 1.09 at R = 3, 1.3 at R = 20). Emerald's 360 ns window is the probe alone, so its idle is likely undercounted.
  - **E2:** the hardware fires less than the priors (0.22–0.86×). The repetition builder still idles after its last round.
  - **E2's d = 10 repetition codes** barely fail: zero-failure points in `bit_0` and `phase_p` (ε ≈ 0), and 0.0021 for `bit_1`, where |1⟩ decays.
- **The timelines.**
  - **Garnet multi-circuit jobs:** execution spans of 16–191 s, which is 362–685 µs per shot for E1, E3, E4 and E5, and 1,418 µs for E2 (circuits up to R = 50, 499 measurements). That is above the 298 µs reset wait plus the circuit, unlike the pilot's single-circuit 50.7 µs. Queues ran 0.6–9.3 s.
  - **Emerald:** about 790 µs per shot (E1) and 505 µs (E4), against the pilot's 1,358 µs at 1,000 shots: consistent with a fixed per-job cost at the pilot.
  - **Wall time:** 26 jobs over 35 min (00:42–01:18 UTC), 1,116 s of server time from created to completed, summed over the jobs.
